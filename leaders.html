The Gap

Algorithmic Empathy — the gap between using AI and thinking with it.

"I've started using AI. But the results still feel... generic. Like answers that could be given to anyone. I know this can be more than that — I just don't know how."

Before Algorithmic Empathy

Question in, generic answer out. AI feels like a smarter search engine — fast, but not contextual. It doesn't know who you are.

After Algorithmic Empathy

AI knows your context, your role, how you want to be answered — without re-explaining every session. Output that feels like it came from someone who already knows you.

Algorithmic Empathy is not a tool. It's a way of thinking alongside AI — deliberately, not by trial and error. This programme is where you build it, using your own real leadership challenge as the material.

Programme Arc

Three phases. One real challenge, fully worked through.

Day 1

Frame & Build

Understand how AI processes context — and why most prompts don't work. Set up AI that "knows" you: instructions, memory, response style. Define the real leadership challenge you're bringing in — and begin working on it. 15 leaders, different challenges, one room.

Midweek

Learning Injection

One shared livestream session — no need to be present in person. A new framework that elevates what you started on Saturday. Your progress from the first session becomes the concrete discussion material.

Day 2

Refine & Transfer

Refine your approach based on what you tried. From challenge to an action plan you can run. Transfer: how to carry this forward to your team and integrate it into daily work. Closing: every leader shares one insight that changed how they see it.

Three outcomes

From "using AI" to "thinking with AI"

  • From "using AI" to "thinking with AI" — your AI skill moves up a level, your way of working changes
  • One real problem, fully worked through — the challenge you bring in, you leave with as an action plan
  • Credibility to lead the transformation — a leader who has done it speaks differently from one who has only read about it
