The Problem

Most people prepare slides. Leaders prepare ideas.

There's a gap between having an idea and being able to sell it. The tools to close that gap now exist. One day to learn how to use them — on your actual work.

🎭
No structure

The idea is clear in your head. It doesn't survive the slide deck — or the room.

⏱
Too dependent

Preparing a presentation takes too many people, too much time, or both.

🔇
Static output

The final deck can't respond to questions, change live, or compute in the room.

The Day

Four modules. One finished presentation from your real work.

M1

Framing — AI as Sceptical Audience

Learn how AI evaluates an idea before an audience does. Use that lens to stress-test your own — before you present it.

M2

Map & Start — 7 Ways AI Presents

Discover seven ways AI can structure and present an idea. Choose the approach that fits your audience. Your real presentation starts here.

M3

Build Artifact — Interactive Prototype

Build a live artifact from your work — one that computes, responds, and can be changed in real time. Not a static deck. A live case.

M4

Slides, Multimedia & Test

Add slides, visual elements, and multimedia. Test and refine until your presentation is ready to deliver — that day.

You leave with

A finished, interactive presentation built from your actual idea

  • A live, interactive artifact — not a static deck — built during the session
  • A reusable AI workflow for any future presentation, proposal, or pitch
  • The ability to prepare, structure, and present your ideas independently — no assistant required
