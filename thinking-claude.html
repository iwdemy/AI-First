The Gap

There is a difference between asking AI for answers and arguing with it.

One Day · Four Blocks · Four Outputs

Build a Claude that already knows who you are.

Block 01

Calibrate — Custom Instructions

Write the instructions that make Claude respond in your context, your voice, your priorities. This is the infrastructure. Everything after compounds from here.

Block 02

Structure — Claude Project

Organise your AI workspace. Upload what Claude needs to know. Build a system that holds context across sessions, not just within them.

Block 03

Connect — 7 Thinking Modes

Decision Support. Strategic Analysis. Long Thinking. Learn to engage AI as a thinking partner, not just a faster search engine. Seven distinct modes — each unlocks a different quality of reasoning.

Block 04

Decide — A Finished Decision from Real Work

Apply everything to one actual decision from your current work. You leave with a real output — not an exercise. Not a sample prompt. Something that was already on your desk.
