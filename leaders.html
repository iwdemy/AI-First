The Gap

You know AI can help.
Applying it to real leadership decisions is different.

You've brought AI into a real leadership problem. The answer came back polished — but generic. It didn't know your team, your organisation, or what was actually at stake. Conviction that AI matters is not the same as being able to think with it.

"I know AI is serious. But when I use it on what actually matters — the output still feels like it could have been written for anyone."

The gap is not scepticism. It is the absence of a method for thinking with AI on problems that require context, judgment, and your specific role.

How AI feels right now

A fast, smart search engine. It answers the question you typed — but not the problem you actually have. Every session begins from zero. AI does not remember you, your role, or what you were working on last week.

What is possible

AI that knows your context, your way of thinking, and how you want to be answered — without you explaining it every time. Output that feels like it came from someone who was already in the meeting with you.

The Distinction

Two kinds of leaders who use AI.
The difference is not a better prompt.

Most leaders

Open AI. Type a problem. Get a generic answer. Try again with different words. Still generic. Close it. Try again next week.

After this programme

Open an AI that already knows your role, your challenge, and the way you think. Start with substance, not setup. The first response already understands who is asking.

The name for this skill is Chat Mastery — the ability to think alongside AI deliberately, not by trial and error. This programme is where you build it.

Chat Mastery is not a tool. It is a way of working. Once built, every conversation becomes faster and more specific — because AI knows who it is talking to.

How It Works

Two Saturdays. Eight modules.
One shift in how you lead with AI.

Every module ends with something you apply — not a concept, but a practice you take back to work.

Saturday 1 · Foundation Mastery

01

From Hype to Reality

Build the right mental model first. What AI can actually do — so your conversations with it start from accuracy, not inflated expectations.

02

Dialogue with AI

The core practice. How to structure a conversation so AI responds to what you actually mean — not just what you typed. Phrasing, context, iteration.

03

Grounded Thinking & Learning

Using dialogue to think, not just to get answers. The patterns that turn AI into a genuine thinking partner — not a search engine.

04

Business Process × AI

Take one real process from your work. Direct AI through it with precision. Leave with a specific workflow — not a general idea to figure out later.

Between Sessions One practice brief: apply what you built on Saturday 1 to a real situation from your work. The cohort reconvenes online — each participant presents their brief, gets feedback, and walks into Saturday 2 already one step ahead.

Saturday 2 · Applied Leadership

05

Personal Knowledge Management

Conversations that capture, organize, and activate what you know. AI handles retrieval so you can focus on the thinking.

06

Writing Companion

Direct AI through conversation to produce finished writing — strategic memos, executive briefs, team comms in your voice. You steer by chat, not configure.

07

AI Creative Partner

The same conversation skills, applied beyond text. Images, visuals, and presentations — multimodal output without going technical.

08

Personalized AI

AI configured for how you specifically work — your context, your patterns, your role. The shift from generic assistant to a tool that already knows what you need.

The design principle

Your work is the curriculum. Not someone else's case study.

  • Every module uses your real context, not a generic example
  • 15 seats — 15 different leaders, working challenges that actually matter
  • Invite only, for AIF alumni — so the room already speaks the same language
What You Gain

Three shifts you keep.

01 — Chat Mastery

You stop asking AI questions. You start directing it with precision — in strategy sessions, in ambiguous calls, in decisions that matter.

02 — Leadership Integration

Your conversations with AI carry real context: your constraints, your stakes, your judgment. AI responds at a different level when you do.

03 — Peer Cohort

Fifteen leaders working the same shift. The room itself becomes a resource you carry forward.

Is This Right for You?

This programme has one entry signal.

Entry Signal

Right for you if: You know AI can help — but when you apply it to real leadership work, the output is still generic. You want a method, not another tool demo.

Not right for you if: You are looking for a prompt library. Those are free and they will not change how you work.

Senior leaders who tried and stalled

You use AI. The output is fast but not contextual. You know this can be more — you just have not found the method yet.

Executives going deeper

You have done multiple AI sessions. You want one programme where you actually apply it to a problem that matters, end to end.

Leaders who need to model it

You are expected to guide your team on AI adoption. To lead it credibly, you need to have done it yourself — not just approved the budget for it.
