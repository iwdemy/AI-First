Day 1 · Foundation

L2.1

AI for You and Me

Where AI creates real value in professional work — and where it does not. No hype, no fear. A clear starting point.

L2.2

Ngobrol dengan AI (ACT→ACTIONS)

The core framework. Give AI a role, context, and precise task — before the question. The difference in output quality is immediate.

L2.3

Grounded Thinking

Anchor AI to your own documents and data using NotebookLM — so it works from what you know, not what it guesses.

L2.4

Business Process & AI

Take one real workflow from your own work. Map it with AI. Leave with something redesigned — not an idea to figure out later.

Between Day 1 & Day 2 One task. Apply the ACTIONS framework to something real that evening. Bring the prompt, the output, and your notes to Day 2.

Common Questions

Things people ask
before they join.

Do I need to be technical to join?

No. This programme is for professionals who work with information, decisions, and communication — not engineers. If you can write a message, you can do this.

How is this different from AI training I've done before?

Most AI training teaches you what to ask. This teaches you how to think — so you can write your own prompts for any situation, not just the examples someone else prepared.

Will I use my own work, or generic case studies?

Your work is the material. Every exercise runs on something you actually do. You don't adapt a stranger's example — you bring the real thing.

What do I walk away with?

A personal prompt system built around three things you do most: writing, analysis, and communication. Not a certificate. A method you use on Monday.

I've tried AI before and it didn't stick. Is this different?

"Tried" usually means: opened it, got a decent answer, moved on. This programme is for people who know AI can do more — and want a method that makes the good results repeatable.
