Why the Gap Persists

An idea that still needs a ghostwriter
isn't really yours yet.

The idea is clear in your head. Making it room-ready takes other people, more drafts, more briefing rounds. Each step adds time and loses nuance.

AI didn't close that gap. It moved the bottleneck one step earlier. Paste in your notes — still get generic output. Because the tool doesn't know the argument you're making.

Every presentation, you brief someone else.
Every meeting, you explain yourself again.

The problem isn't the slide software. It's that no one in the chain knows how you think.

The Distinction

Two kinds of leaders.
Same room. The difference isn't the tool.

Most leaders

Brief a designer. Wait for a draft. Revise. Brief again. Enter the room with slides that don't quite sound like you — and can't respond when questions go off script.

After this day

Bring your idea. Build it into a live interactive presentation during the session. Leave with something that responds to the room — ready to deliver this week.

The difference is a workflow. And seven presentation modes — one for every room you walk into.

What Happens

Four modules. One finished presentation
from your actual work.

Each module builds on the last. You leave with a finished presentation — not notes.

M1

Framing — AI as Sceptical Audience

AI becomes your first sceptical audience. One live demo shows what a strong argument looks like. You map your communication moments using ACTIONS, then match each to the right mode.

M2

Map & Decide — Seven Ways to Present with AI

Seven modes mapped across prep speed, engagement, post-session reach, and total effort. No single mode wins all four. You choose which fits the room — then build from your own idea.

M3

Build Artifact — Interactive Prototype

Build a live Artifact using Gemini Canvas — something that computes, responds to questions, and updates in real time. From your own work. Done by end of M3.

M4

Deliver & Test — Perform, Respond, Leave Behind

Deliver with the live Artifact. Simulate objections — respond in the room, not after. Add supporting slides, see a Multimedia demo. Leave tested and ready.
